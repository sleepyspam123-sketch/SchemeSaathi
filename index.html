Schemes are decided at the counter, in two minutes

A kirana owner gets several distributor schemes a week: 10+1 free, 2% off for cash, ₹15 off per case above 5 cases, buy ₹25,000 this month and get 3% back. The salesman is waiting, customers are at the counter, and the decision is made on gut feel.

The usual mistake is over-buying to catch the scheme. The discount is real, but the stock sits for months and the cash that should have bought fast-moving items is locked in cartons at the back of the shop.

“Scheme ke chakkar mein maal pada reh gaya.”

The scheme got me, and the stock just sat there. A kirana owner in our pilot.

How it works

No forms to learn. Paste what the salesman sent and answer one question you already know the answer to.

  1. Paste the scheme

    Exactly as it arrived, in Hinglish, with abbreviations like CD, ctn and pc. If something important is missing, SchemeSaathi asks one short question.

  2. Tell us your usual buy

    How many cases you normally take and how long they last. Owners remember this better than “weekly sales”, so that is what we ask.

  3. Get the answer

    How many cases to buy, cash or credit, how much extra cash gets locked up and for how long, and a short verdict in simple Hinglish.

What comes back

  • How many cases to buy. A quantity, not a lecture on margins.
  • Cash or credit. A 2% cash discount for paying about 14 days early is worth roughly 53% a year. Most owners never see it that way.
  • Cash locked up, and for how long. The number that actually hurts when it goes wrong.
  • A verdict in Hinglish. Two or three lines, the way a trusted accountant would say it.

Questions

Is SchemeSaathi against distributors?

No. It helps owners buy the right quantity. Shops that don’t get stuck with slow stock pay on time and keep ordering.

Is it on WhatsApp?

Pilot shops use it on WhatsApp today. Everyone else can use the web version, and WhatsApp access opens to the waitlist in batches.

Can I send a photo of the scheme?

Yes, on WhatsApp you can send a photo of a printed scheme or rate sheet. Handwritten sheets work most of the time; if something is unclear, SchemeSaathi asks.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the scheme you paste and the usual buy you tell us. The AI reads the scheme; the calculations are done by plain code, and every assumption is shown with the answer.

What happens to my data?

Your schemes and purchase details are used to give you answers and remember your usual buy. We do not sell your data to brands or distributors.
